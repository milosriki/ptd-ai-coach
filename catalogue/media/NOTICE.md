# Exercise media — MIT License

All non-commercial Gym visual assets from upstream openGym have been permanently removed.

Every exercise in the 5,632-exercise catalogue uses 100% commercial-legal MIT-licensed artwork:

1. **Human Exercise Loops (3,464 files):** Stills (`still/*.webp`) and animated loops (`clip/*.mp4`)
   derived from **apoorvdarshan/fud-ai** (`shared/workout-vectors/`), authored by Apoorv Darshan
   and released under the **MIT License** (commercial use, modification, and redistribution permitted).
2. **Anatomical Muscle Map Diagrams (3,090 files):** Per-exercise male/female Front + Back anatomical
   muscle highlight stills (`still/*.webp`) and activation-pulse loops (`clip/*.mp4`) generated from
   the MIT-licensed anatomical body geometry (`frontend/src/lib/body-geometry.js`, MuscleMap MIT).
